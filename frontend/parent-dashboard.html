💡 Understanding Cohort Percentile Rank

How peer comparisons are computed without individual learner exposure.

The cohort percentile indicates your child’s relative standing among all peers in the same grade level and stream based on Period 1 assessments. Using the standard statistical midpoint rank formula, a percentile of 70th means your child performed as well as or higher than 70% of learners in their class cohort. Percentiles provide contextual insight beyond raw percentages.
